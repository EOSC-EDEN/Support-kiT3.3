A Core Preservation Process (CPP) is a specific action that every Trustworthy Digital Archive should undertake adequately, either directly or through associated parties or services. The 30 CPPs are defined by [EOSC EDEN WP1](https://eosc-eden.github.io/wp1-cpp-descriptions/). Each page below shows the process, how it relates to other processes, how it maps to certification frameworks, and **which modules of this kit teach it**.
